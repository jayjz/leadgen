# Experiment design
1. Freeze hypothesis, buyer, payer, channel, scope, denominator and exclusion rules **before** fieldwork.
2. Verify access, ability to pay, written fee/offer terms and blockers before mining demand.
3. Specify hard time/cash ceilings, consent/approval points and no-go conditions.
4. Define primary outcome as independently verified action or settled operator payment; track intermediate stages separately.
5. Record all attempts, failures, staff time, incremental cash costs, rework, missing observations and alternative explanations.
6. Precommit continue/stop/pivot decisions. Do not call one transaction a repeatable business or claim causal lift without a comparison.
7. Require explicit authorization before outreach, account access, purchases or publishing.
