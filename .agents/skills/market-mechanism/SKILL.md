# Market mechanism analysis
1. Map buyer, supplier, operator, channel gatekeeper and money flow.
2. Identify the job-to-be-done and where a transaction fails despite supply.
3. Name who pays and **why**, with verifiable terms rather than hypothetical referral rates.
4. Evaluate free substitutes, existing brokers, Meta-native features, access fragility and regulatory boundaries.
5. Rank testability separately from expected economics, repeatability and defensibility.
6. Recommend the smallest payer-first test; name the strongest evidence that would refute it.
